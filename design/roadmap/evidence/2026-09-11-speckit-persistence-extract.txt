# Spec Persistence Models | Spec Kit Documentation
# saved 2026-09-11 from https://github.github.com/spec-kit/concepts/spec-persistence.html

Spec Kit intentionally leaves teams in control of what happens to spec.md,
plan.md, and tasks.md after requirements change.

Spec-first: write a spec before coding, then allow it to be discarded.

Living spec: spec.md is the contract and the other artifacts are derived from it.
Teams update spec.md first and then regenerate or revise plan.md and tasks.md
from that source. The plan and task list are still valuable, but they are
treated as disposable derivations rather than permanent sources of truth.
